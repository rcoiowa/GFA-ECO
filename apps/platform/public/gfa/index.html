How we walk alongside you

Support for the life
you want to build.

Someone to listen. Space to grow. Help making the next step feel possible.

Our non-clinical support begins with your strengths, your goals, and what matters to you. We can help you connect with other services when you need them.

Recovery capital means the relationships, resources, skills, and opportunities that help people build stability and a meaningful life.
Find your starting point ↗
Life transformation & recovery coaching

Explore your goals, build on your strengths, and work toward practical changes with someone walking alongside you. Ask about coaching.

Peer support & mentoring

Connect through shared understanding and lived experience. Make room for honest conversation, encouragement, and your own path forward. Connect with GFA.

Recovery circles & community

Find connection through peer-led conversations that honor different recovery pathways and permission-based feedback. Ask about current circles and meeting times.

Resource navigation & warm handoffs

Talk through needs such as housing, employment, transportation, and community resources. GFA helps you identify options and connect with appropriate support. Ask for navigation support.

Recovery Ally & wraparound support

Build a circle of encouragement and practical connection around your goals. Ask how an ally or community partner can help.

Reentry & the Live-Out Program

Explore support for returning to the community, building routines, and working toward stability. The Live-Out Program offers structure and accountability while participants live independently. Ask about current participation requirements.

Warmline & check-ins

Reach out for peer support and connection at 515-310-3425 (515-310-DIAL). This is not an emergency service. See all support options.

Our purpose

Connection
Prevents Crisis.

This principle guides our work: make connection easier, honor people’s dignity, and help build support before challenges become harder to carry alone.

Grace For Addictions is a peer-led 501(c)(3) Recovery Community Organization based in Des Moines, Iowa. We provide free, non-clinical recovery support, life transformation and recovery coaching, connection, mentoring, and resource navigation.

We walk alongside people and families impacted by substance use, mental health challenges, justice involvement, housing instability, and other barriers to lasting stability.

Our grace-based roots shape how we welcome people. Participation does not require religious belief or practice. We honor multiple pathways to recovery.
