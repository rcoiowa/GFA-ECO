Contact Connect

The form asks for your name, a way to contact you, a reason for connecting, and an optional short message. Your permission to be contacted is recorded with the inquiry. It does not enroll you in marketing or create an account.

Requests are sent to GFA’s RecoveryOS lead queue, hosted in Supabase. Access is limited by the application’s staff permissions. A minimal staff notification may identify that a request has arrived; the form’s message and contact details are not included in external email notifications.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks the submission for automated abuse. Do not include medical records, identification numbers, financial details, or another person’s private history.

Questions or changes

For questions about your information, or to request a correction or ask about deletion, contact connect@graceforaddictions.org or 515-220-8771. This notice describes Contact Connect; other services may have their own consent processes.

Contact Connect is not continuously monitored and is not an emergency service.
