Before you apply

Confirm the residence you selected. Review its current information and ask the intake team about required documents, medication policies, meeting expectations, fees, and the decision process.

Use the residence-specific application once. Follow the instructions shown for any account or follow-up steps. If you already applied or are unsure which residence fits, contact the office before submitting again.

Returning to the community?

GFA can help you explore peer support, resource navigation, and connections around your goals. Ask about reentry support or the Live-Out Program if you are living independently and looking for structure and accountability.

You can connect with GFA without applying for housing.
