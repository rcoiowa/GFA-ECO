What happens next?

  1. Share a starting point.

    Tell us how to contact you and what you are looking for. It’s okay to say you’re not sure.

  2. Explore what fits.

    A conversation helps clarify your goals, current options, and any program requirements.

  3. Choose a next step.

    That may be GFA support, a community resource, or a connection with another provider.

I need organizational documents.

Contact the office to request current organizational documents, bylaws, or other published information.

I’m supporting someone I care about.

You deserve support, too. Reach out about your own questions and needs. You do not need to disclose someone else’s private history to begin.

I’m a referral or community partner.

Call the office at 515-220-8771 to discuss fit and the appropriate referral process. Confirm the person’s permission before sharing their information. A referral is not an admission decision.

I want to volunteer, become an ally, or partner.

Use Contact Connect or call the office. Tell us about your interests and availability. We can discuss current opportunities, preparation, and expectations.

Do I need an account or a particular belief?

No account or religious belief is required to ask about support. GFA honors multiple recovery pathways. Some programs have their own participation requirements, which should be explained before you enroll.
