## Partnership discovery worksheet
For each organization: public name and URL; Iowa geography; existing services; current technology; self-identified access gap; proposed complementary support; owner/contact path from public source; consent for follow-up; next human-approved step. Research is not an endorsement or partnership agreement. Keep personal correspondence out of this repository.

## Metric definitions for review
- Inquiry response time: elapsed time from valid inquiry received to first confirmed human response; show median and eligible count, exclude spam/tests, report unresolved count separately.
- Organizations completing onboarding: distinct approved organizations that meet the agreed access checklist in the period; exclude inquiries, invitations and demonstrations.
- Active organizations: distinct approved organizations with a defined confirmed operational event in the reporting period; agree the event definition before publishing a count.
- Access barriers resolved: distinct reported barriers with a human-confirmed resolution; show open barriers separately and do not equate resolution with recovery outcome.
- Delivered services: only existing authorized human/system-confirmed service events; never infer from website views or agent activity.

Baseline values: unavailable. No fabricated zeros or statewide coverage claims.
