# Decision: Ratification of GFA AI Governance Documents v1.0

Recorded per §14 (Decision Logging) of the GFA Project Knowledge Authority & Conflict
Resolution Protocol.

- **Decision:** Ratify the following two documents as Grace For Addictions' canonical
  internal operating standards:
  1. **GFA Project Knowledge Authority & Conflict Resolution Protocol v1.0**
     (`docs/governance/gfa-project-knowledge-authority-protocol-v1.0.md`)
  2. **GFA AI Work Request & Prompting Standard v1.0**
     (`docs/governance/gfa-ai-work-request-standard-v1.0.md`)
- **Date:** September 3, 2026
- **Decision-maker:** Executive Director, Grace For Addictions (Thomas), recorded through
  an authenticated, executive-directed working session.
- **Rationale:** GFA's AI-assisted work spans knowledge governance, technical systems,
  communications, and program operations across multiple AI systems. These standards
  establish a single authority hierarchy, evidence discipline, conflict-resolution
  procedure, and work-request structure so that AI-assisted work is auditable,
  evidence-honest, appropriately scoped, and implemented only when authorized.
- **Conditions:** Both documents remain subordinate to applicable law, GFA's Articles of
  Incorporation, GFA's Bylaws, and any valid Board resolution or board-reserved authority.
  Within the documents' own hierarchy, this subordination corresponds to Level 3
  (Primary / Legal Authority) controlling where those instruments govern the issue.
- **Revisit trigger:** Adoption of a superseding version (v1.1+), a Board action touching
  AI governance, or a material conflict discovered between these standards and Level 3
  authority.
- **Superseded decision:** None. These are the first ratified versions; earlier partial
  drafts circulated in working sessions are historical material (Level 8) reconciled by
  this ratification.
