# Public resource directory review — October 5, 2026

Compared the ten-entry PR #26 directory with the uploaded January 3, 2026 “First Stop After Prison” guide and the uploaded “Comprehensive Iowa Recovery Ecosystem and Virtual Support Network Database” text. Official source pages were reviewed before adding entries.

## Result
- Added 20 entries; 30 directory entries total.
- Added categories: Reentry, Online meetings, Healthcare, Harm reduction.
- Original Iowa 211, Your Life Iowa, Recovery Iowa, NAMI Iowa, HHS, IowaWORKS, Iowa Legal Aid, and RecoveryOS entries retained once each.
- One entry per listed provider/network, with locations described rather than repeated as provider cards.
- Automated integrity checks reject duplicate IDs, names, and normalized destinations (ignoring www and trailing slashes).
- A link is navigation, not a completed connection, referral, availability guarantee, or RecoveryOS service event.

## Corrections and consolidation
- ORS is Iowa Office of Recovery Services, not “Outreach Recovery System” and not an alias for Recovery Iowa.
- Full Circle/Recover Full Circle and its multiple locations use one canonical provider entry.
- NA uses the official NA World Services virtual finder; no second overlapping Virtual NA card.
- MA uses the official Marijuana Anonymous finder instead of an additional MA Online listing.
- Existing 988 and warmline contacts remain under Support Now; no duplicate crisis provider cards.
- Iowa HHS services remain under one assistance entry rather than repeating SNAP and Medicaid cards.

## Held for further verification
- Secular AA separate listing: authoritative current destination not verified in this pass; secular options are represented by LifeRing and SMART.
- Regional crisis access-center contacts, mobile-dispatch coverage, clinical admission promises, FQHC classifications, fees, and schedules in the pasted synthesis were not imported without individual current verification.
- Detailed reentry guide provider listings remain accessible through Living Beyond the Bars instead of copying all addresses, eligibility restrictions, and personal contacts into RecoveryOS.
