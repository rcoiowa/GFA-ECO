## Homepage introduction

Grace For Addictions is a peer-led recovery community organization based in Des Moines, Iowa. We help people explore human peer recovery support, coaching, recovery circles, resource navigation, reentry support, and recovery residence support. Contact us to ask about current options and program requirements.

We honor different recovery pathways and meet people with dignity, accountability, and grace.

No Shame, No Stigma, Just Grace.

## Online community and access

Our online community is designed to make recovery information and opportunities for connection easier to find. Explore available resources and ways to ask for human support. A resource listing or submitted request does not confirm that a service is available or that a connection has been completed.

## Support during transitions

Leaving treatment, returning from incarceration, or facing a change in housing can bring practical questions. GFA can help you explore recovery supports and a next step within the services currently available. Human peer support is distinct from clinical treatment and emergency response. For urgent help, use the site's support options; do not wait for a contact-form reply.

## Learning and recovery

Stress, habits, routines, relationships, and living circumstances can affect a person's experience of recovery. We ask what would help, explain expectations clearly, and offer practical support within the program's scope. We do not assume the cause of a person's behavior or promise neurological changes from a service.

## Coaching and service inquiries

You are welcome to ask for support related to substance use, recovery, or supporting someone you care about. Contact the team to discuss current coaching and navigation options, who each service is for, any costs, and participation requirements. Each person's goals and preferred recovery pathway matter.

## Grace description where Grace is offered

Grace, AI Support Navigator, is designed to help people explore options, find approved recovery resources, and choose whether to contact a real person. Grace is AI and has no lived experience. Describe only the functions available in that channel, keep the participant disclosure visible, and keep urgent support accessible independently of AI.

## Form and confirmation requirements

- Explain required fields and why information is needed; do not add demographic or health-data collection under this editorial change.
- Use “request received” only when the receiver confirms receipt. Do not say that a staff member has read it, a message was delivered, an appointment occurred, or a connection was completed without confirmation of that exact state.
- For an uncertain response, retain entries and explain that receipt could not be confirmed. Provide the established contact alternative.
- Keep marketing permission separate from service contact and story/photo permission.
- Review accessibility labels, validation messages, page titles, SEO descriptions, mobile copy, translated pages, FAQs, service descriptions, and existing blog/CMS content against the same guide.
