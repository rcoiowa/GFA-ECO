# EJWRH Resident Rights & Grievance Process

Ernest & Johnnie White Recovery House · 1414 12th Street, Des Moines, Iowa 50314
Office: 515-220-8771 · ejwrh@rcoiowa.org

These rights are yours from the day you apply. Nothing you sign at EJWRH reduces them.

## Your rights

1. **Dignity and respect.** You are a person in recovery, never a case, a diagnosis, or a
   history. No shame. No stigma. Just grace.
2. **Freedom from discrimination** — including your recovery pathway and your use of
   prescribed medications for opioid or alcohol use disorder (MOUD). Taking your prescribed
   medication is never a violation and never grounds for different treatment.
3. **Your own care.** You choose your clinical providers. Nobody at the residence makes
   medical decisions for you or requires you to change medications.
4. **Safety.** A home free of alcohol, illicit drugs, violence, and harassment.
5. **Your information.** What is collected about you is the minimum needed to support you.
   You can see your own records. Information leaves the organization only with your
   specific, written, revocable authorization — or where the law requires it.
6. **Honest process.** Decisions that affect your residency — including anything following a
   screening result — are made by people, explained to you, and documented. No automated
   decision ends anyone's residency.
7. **Your voice.** You can raise concerns, file grievances, and participate in house
   decisions without fear of retaliation.
8. **Your belongings.** Personal property is respected and handled per the Handbook,
   including when residency ends.

## Your responsibilities

Respect the same rights in others: housemates' dignity, safety, privacy, and property; the
house commitments in your Participant & Residency Agreement; and honesty with the people
walking beside you.

## The grievance process

1. **Say it.** Raise the concern with any staff member you trust — in person, in writing, or
   through the app. If it concerns that person, go to the house manager or any Grace For
   Addictions staff member instead.
2. **It gets written down.** Staff record the grievance the day it is received. You get a
   copy on request.
3. **It gets answered.** The house manager (or, where the grievance concerns the manager,
   organizational leadership) reviews it with you and responds in writing within
   [PENDING — OPERATOR: response window, e.g. 5 business days].
4. **You can appeal.** If the response does not resolve it, you may appeal to
   [PENDING — OPERATOR/GFA: named appeal contact], who reviews it fresh.
5. **No retaliation — ever.** Filing a grievance can never be used against you in phase
   decisions, passes, screening responses, or residency decisions.

Outside contacts are always available to you, including the Iowa affiliate (MCRSP) for
recovery-residence standards concerns.
