# EJWRH Medication & MOUD Policy

Ernest & Johnnie White Recovery House · 1414 12th Street, Des Moines, Iowa 50314

## The commitment first

**Medication for opioid or alcohol use disorder (MOUD/MAT) — buprenorphine, methadone,
naltrexone, and others prescribed to you — is recovery, not a compromise of it.** Taking
your prescribed medication is never a violation, never grounds for denial or discharge, and
never treated differently from any other prescription. Nobody at EJWRH will pressure you to
change, taper, or stop a medication — those decisions belong to you and your prescriber.

## How medications are handled in the house

- At intake, staff review your current medications with you and record the minimum: the
  medication name, how it is stored, and whether a prescriber is on file. **No dosages, no
  diagnoses, and no medical history are collected.** If you take no medications, staff
  simply record that the review happened.
- Storage classes: **self-managed** (you keep and manage it), **secure storage** (locked
  storage with staff access process), and **staff count** (stored with periodic counts) —
  assigned per medication based on safety, not on what the medication is for.
  [PENDING — OPERATOR: which classes the house uses by default and the controlled-medication
  storage procedure.]
- Keep medications in original containers, and don't share or hold anyone else's.
- New prescriptions or changes: tell staff so the record stays current — that's the whole
  ask.

## If something goes missing

Report lost or missing medication to staff immediately. Missing controlled medication is a
safety issue for the whole house and is documented and handled without assuming fault.
