# EJWRH Participant & Residency Agreement

Ernest & Johnnie White Recovery House · 1414 12th Street, Des Moines, Iowa 50314
Office: 515-220-8771 · ejwrh@rcoiowa.org

> **About this agreement.** This is a program participation agreement, not a residential
> lease. It is the one document you sign to live at EJWRH. Read it carefully, and ask
> questions about anything unclear before signing — asking first is exactly what intake is
> for. Signing is a mutual commitment: the house commits to you as you commit to the house.
> **Nothing in this agreement waives your rights as a resident.**

This agreement references the following documents, which you will review and acknowledge —
they explain, they do not add hidden contract terms: the **Resident Handbook**, the
**Resident Rights & Grievance Process**, the **Screening Policy**, the **Medication & MOUD
Policy**, and the **Return-to-Use Response Policy**.

## 1. Who we are and what you can expect

EJWRH is a recovery residence operated by the Ernest & Johnnie White Recovery House. Recovery
support services — peer coaching, recovery navigation, recovery planning, and the RecoveryOS
platform — are provided by Grace For Addictions as contracted services. The residence is
pursuing NARR Level II certification through the Iowa affiliate (MCRSP).

You can expect: a safe, alcohol- and illicit-drug-free home; respect and dignity without
regard to your recovery pathway; staff and peers who walk beside you; and honest, documented
decisions — never automated ones — about anything that affects your residency.

## 2. Your residency

- Residency begins on your admission date and continues while you participate in the program
  and meet the commitments in this agreement.
- This is program housing: rooms and beds are assigned by the house and may change with notice.
- Guests, overnight passes, and quiet hours follow the Resident Handbook.

## 3. The three-phase program

Your residency moves through three phases. Advancement is a decision made **with** you by
staff — it is based on your stability and readiness, never an automatic timer.

| Phase | Focus | Curfew |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Foundation | Stabilize, orient, connect, build your plan | 9:00 PM |
| 2 — Growth | Consistency, responsibility, recovery capital | 10:00 PM Sun–Thu / 11:00 PM Fri–Sat |
| 3 — Leadership & Transition | Independence, mentorship, transition | 11:00 PM Sun–Thu / Midnight Fri–Sat |

Curfew never extends past midnight in any phase.

## 4. Participation commitments

- **Work, school, training, or volunteering:** once you are stabilized — a judgment made with
  staff, not a clock — the target is **30 hours per week** of employment, education,
  training, or volunteering combined.
- **Recovery support:** qualifying recovery-support activities each week — **4 per week in
  Phase 1, 3 per week in Phase 2, 2 per week in Phase 3.** Any approved pathway counts:
  mutual-aid meetings of any tradition, SMART Recovery, faith-based recovery, cultural
  practices, peer support, and recovery coaching sessions all count. **No specific pathway
  is ever required.** A sponsor or mentor relationship is strongly encouraged and never
  mandatory.
- **House life:** house meetings, assigned chores, and the community expectations in the
  Resident Handbook.

## 6. A substance-free home: screening

You agree to participate in the drug and alcohol screening described in the Screening Policy.
Two protections are permanent:

- **Medications you are lawfully prescribed — including MOUD (such as buprenorphine,
  methadone, or naltrexone) — are never a violation.** A screening result consistent with
  your prescription is lawful medication, not a program issue.
- **A positive or concerning result never automatically ends your residency.** What happens
  next always begins with a care conversation (see Section 7).

Your authorization for screening is recorded as a consent you can see in your RecoveryOS
account; the Screening Policy explains the process.

## 7. If return to use happens

Return to use is met with care first. The sequence is always: a **care conversation** with
staff → assessment of your immediate safety and circumstances → intensified support or
referral where appropriate → an honest look at community safety and fit → a **documented
human decision**. Possible outcomes include staying with added support, a modified support
plan, temporary transition to a higher level of care, or — as a last resort and never
automatically — discharge. No screening result, by itself, discharges anyone.

## 8. Nondiscrimination

EJWRH and Grace For Addictions do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion,
national origin, sex, disability, familial status, or recovery pathway — **including the use
of prescribed medications for opioid or alcohol use disorder.** MOUD is never grounds for
denial, discharge, or different treatment.

## 9. Safety and conduct

You commit to: no alcohol or illicit drugs in the home; no violence or threats; no weapons;
honesty with staff about safety concerns; and respect for housemates, staff, and neighbors.
Serious safety violations can end residency — through the documented human decision process,
with your rights (Section 10) intact.

## 10. Your rights

Your rights are stated in the Resident Rights & Grievance Process document and are not
reduced by this agreement. They include dignity and respect, freedom from discrimination,
your own clinical providers and medication decisions, access to your own records, and a
grievance process without retaliation.

## 11. Moving out

Planned transitions are worked as part of your recovery plan — staff help you plan the next
step, and the exit process in the Resident Handbook applies. If residency ends for cause, it
follows the documented-decision process above. Personal belongings are handled respectfully
per the Handbook.

## 12. Records and privacy

Your participation records live in RecoveryOS. What is collected is the minimum needed to
support you; who can see it is limited by role; information leaves the organization only
with your specific, revocable, written authorization or as required by law.

## 13. Signing

By signing you confirm you have read this agreement, had your questions answered, received
the referenced documents, and agree to these terms.
