# EJWRH Screening Policy

Ernest & Johnnie White Recovery House · 1414 12th Street, Des Moines, Iowa 50314

Screening exists to keep the home safe for everyone's recovery — it is a safety practice,
not a trap. This policy explains how it works; your separate screening consent (which you
can see in your account) is what authorizes it.

## How screening works

- Screening may be **random**, and may otherwise be conducted under this approved policy
  (including for a safety concern staff can articulate).
- A test may be requested and administered only by the **House Manager**, the **designated
  Liaison**, or **Executive Leadership** — no one else.
- Approved methods: **urine screen (UA)**, **breathalyzer**, and **oral-fluid (saliva)**
  testing. Screening is conducted respectfully and privately.

## Refusing or missing a test

Directly refusing a properly communicated test, or failing to appear for one without a
reasonable explanation, may be treated as a refusal — which starts a conversation and is
documented, never an automatic consequence. **Legitimate temporary unavailability — being at
work, at an appointment, or otherwise genuinely unreachable — is not a refusal** and is never
automatically labeled one.

## If you dispute a result

A nonnegative result you contest can be checked, under this policy and at resident expense:

- **immediate repeat testing** where appropriate; and/or
- **confirmatory laboratory testing.**

While verification is pending, the result is recorded as **contested / unconfirmed** — not
as a positive — and no residency decision is made from it.

## What results mean — three permanent protections

1. **Prescribed medication is never a violation.** A result consistent with a medication you
   are lawfully prescribed — including MOUD such as buprenorphine, methadone, or naltrexone —
   is lawful medication. It is not a positive result for program purposes.
2. **No result automatically ends residency.** A positive or concerning result always leads
   first to a care conversation: how are you, what happened, what do you need. Then an
   assessment of immediate safety, intensified support or clinical referral where it fits,
   an honest look at community safety and continued fit, and a **documented human decision**
   — facilitated by the Operator, the designated Liaison, and/or authorized Executive
   Leadership. Outcomes range from staying with more support, to a modified plan or a
   temporary higher level of care, to — as a last resort — discharge.
3. **Results are private.** Screening results are visible only to the roles with an
   operational need. They are **never posted in GroupMe**, never put in general coaching
   notes, and leave the organization only under applicable authority or your specific
   written consent — with an audit record either way.

## Your consent

Your authorization to participate in screening is recorded as a consent grant at intake.
You may revoke it at any time by telling staff — revocation is honored going forward and
starts a conversation about whether the program can still work for you; it is never punished
and never triggers an automatic discharge.
