# REVIEW_ORDER — recommended reading order

Read the package in this order. The first six give you the whole picture and every open decision; the rest are the detailed backing.

1. **00_EXECUTIVE_COMPARISON.md** — the two residences side by side; verdicts.
2. **17_LATISHA_REQUEST_MAP.md** — every requested item, mapped per residence with status and where it lives.
3. **18_READINESS_SHEETS_AND_OCTOBER12.md** — one-page readiness per residence + the de-identified October-12 human verification checklist + the corrected verdict.
4. **15_DECISION_AND_ACTIVATION_REGISTER.md** — the activation-state ladder and the decisions required from Thomas (D1–D27).
5. **16_GAP_AND_CONFLICT_REPORT.md** — conflicts (not silently resolved) and the gap register.
6. **09_AUTHORITY_ROLES_AND_ACCESS_MATRIX.md** — who may decide what, per residence; Latisha/Yvette authority is UNKNOWN — AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED.
7. **11_EMERGENCY_CONTACT_ROI_AND_REFERRAL_MATRIX.md** — the communication-type taxonomy that governs referral/ROI questions across the package.
8. **03_EJWRH_OPERATIONS_PACKET_INDEX.md** and **04_GRACE_HOUSE_OPERATIONS_PACKET_INDEX.md** — the per-residence operations indexes.
9. **Remaining detailed files:** 01 (shared standards), 02 (controlled document register), 05/06 (inquiry-to-move-in checklists), 07 (eligibility & documentation), 08 (bed inventory & hold), 10 (email & communication), 12 (application & domain), 13 (transition-readiness template), 14 (training & implementation).

**Reading conventions used throughout**
- Database records ≠ physical reality. A 0-row RecoveryOS inventory is a *data gap*, not proof of physical state; capacity `null`/`12` are configured values, not physical counts; "no real applicant proven" ≠ "no applications ever received."
- Document-state vocabulary is defined once in **02** and used consistently: `active/published` never implies `assigned`, `distributed`, `trained`, or `operationally used`.
- EJWRH admission authority is stated as a governing role assigned by PSA v4.1, with operational reliance **contingent** on verifying the executed PSA and the Operator incumbent.
- Occupancy characterization and Iowa Code ch. 562A are **subject to counsel review**; document terminology is not a settled legal conclusion.
