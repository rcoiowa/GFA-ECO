# Proposed statewide direction

Design discussion supplied by Thomas, October 2, 2026; recorded here for implementation planning. This is not a claim of statewide partner enrollment or a deployment instruction.

**RCO Iowa · Iowa’s Recovery Community Center**

A shared digital front door to recovery connection, support, and community.
Founded and operated by Grace For Addictions. Powered by RecoveryOS.

RCOIowa.org is proposed as the statewide gathering place; Iowa’s Recovery Community Center is the public experience; RecoveryOS supports continuity after someone enters. The goal is accessible recovery connection across urban and rural communities while preserving independent partner identities and participant choice.

## Proposed domain roles

| Domain | Role |
| --- | --- |
| rcoiowa.org | Primary public home for the initiative and virtual center |
| recoverycommunity.center | Memorable entrance to the same public experience |
| recoverycommunity.app | Account-based continuity and authorized work |

No domain or DNS changes are included.

## Public and partner experience

- Front desk: an accountable recipient, contact method and clear next step.
- Kiosk: resource discovery, circles, learning and temporary planning without mandatory registration.
- Community board: listings with an organizer, verification date and cancellation state.
- Organization directory: actual services, eligibility, location and availability.
- Optional account: continuity, saved choices, reminders and consent-based ongoing support.
- Partner workspace: scoped responsibilities and documentation, preserving organization boundaries.

Suggested participation options are listed partner, connection partner and service partner using RecoveryOS. These are proposals, not adopted roles or quality rankings. Directory participation must not automatically grant access to participant records. Define listing ownership, request acknowledgment, follow-through, correction, and access responsibilities before onboarding.

## Evaluation discussion for Public Science Collaborative

Proposed question: can an accessible shared digital front door, connected to accountable human follow-through, reduce the effort of finding support and strengthen continuity of connection?

Explore reach, accessibility, meaningful connection, continuity, and barriers with participants and partners. Define a bounded pilot. Distinguish visits, requests, acknowledged connections, actual participation, and follow-through. Do not infer service delivery from page views or AI interactions. Research access requires its own agreed process; routine service information is not automatically available for research.

Describe the initiative as building a statewide virtual recovery community center. Claims of being Iowa’s first or having statewide partner coverage require verification.

## Scope of this code snapshot

The visual prototype reflects the October 2 logo and typography revision. This document preserves the subsequent statewide discussion; organization directory and partner workflows are not implemented in this snapshot. Guest account previews and meeting receipts remain simulated. Real community circle logging requires approved scoped permissions, occurrence records, anonymous headcount handling, optional authorized named attendance, corrections and deduplication.
